Questions and answers

Do you make commercial film?

Yes, I make commercial and campaign film for brands and agencies. I direct, run the camera, edit and colour grade myself.

Are you a production company?

No, I am a videographer and director working with a small team. It suits you when you want a short route from idea to finished film.

Do you shoot both stills and film?

Yes, I shoot both stills and film in the same production, and I edit and colour grade the film myself. The KIA EV6 campaign was shot that way.

Do you edit and grade yourself?

Yes, I edit and colour grade my own films. Both are done in-house.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes, I travel for shoots. I am based in Stockholm and work wherever the job is.
