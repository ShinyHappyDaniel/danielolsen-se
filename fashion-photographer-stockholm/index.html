I'm Daniel Olsén, a fashion photographer in Stockholm. I shoot fashion for brands, agencies and magazines: campaigns, lookbooks, editorials and e-commerce, from a small team to a full production.

I am based in Stockholm and work wherever the job is.

What I shoot

  • Brand and seasonal campaigns
  • Lookbooks and catalogues
  • Editorials for magazines, including RES, Daisy Beauty Magazine and Metro Mode
  • Fashion and lifestyle content for social and web

How I work

  • I plan with you before the day: references, casting, locations and crew.
  • On set I keep it calm and fast, so the model and the team can do their best work.
  • You get a clear edit and retouched files that fit your channels.

Questions and answers

Do you shoot both stills and film?

Yes, I shoot both stills and film in the same production, and I edit and colour grade the film myself. The KIA EV6 campaign was shot that way.

Do you do your own retouch?

Yes, I retouch my own images. Retouch, editing and colour grading are all done in-house.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes, I travel for shoots. I am based in Stockholm and work wherever the job is. Recent campaigns have been shot in Skåne and in Cape Town, South Africa.

What can you and your team take care of?

My team and I can take care of everything from location scouting and model booking to retouch and editing.

What is included in a fashion shoot?

A fashion shoot includes preparation, a selection, retouch and finished files. I plan the job with you before the shoot, you pick the final images from a selection, I retouch them and deliver files that fit your channels.

How do usage rights work for a fashion shoot?

Usage rights for a fashion shoot are agreed per job. Channels, period and market are written into the quote.

What does a fashion shoot cost?

The price of a fashion shoot depends on the scope of the job. You get a quote once I have seen the brief.

How far ahead do I need to book a fashion shoot?

It varies. Sometimes a fashion shoot can be booked at short notice, but get in touch as early as you can.
