Tonje Halvorsen: clothing made of safety pins

The amazing contemporary artist Tonje Halvorsen makes clothing items out of safety pins. I am so happy to have the opportunity to capture her art. Hopefully a lot more to come.

The series shows several of her pieces on models: a see-through jacket, a vest, a face mask with pearls and a full bridal gown made of white safety pins, photographed both in studio and in an autumn park.
