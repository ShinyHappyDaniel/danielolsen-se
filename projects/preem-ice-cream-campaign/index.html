Preem "Ice Cream": summer campaign images

Contracted by Åkestam Holst NOA to shoot the images for Preem’s summer ice cream campaign, in Stockholm in 2024.

Two models in loud summer outfits, each with an ice cream cone, photographed at a Preem station. The images were used as finished ads with headline and price.

Models: Chaya and Jeffry. Make-up by Mirja Andersson, styling by Anna Göransson.
