Milles: restaurant content for social media

Making a social media platform, like Instagram, stand out can be tricky. Here is my take on the high-end restaurant Milles’ content.

The pictures are shot at the table, in the middle of dinner: oysters and champagne on green marble, friends leaning in, candles and dark walls. Playful rather than polished, made to feel like a night you want to be part of.
