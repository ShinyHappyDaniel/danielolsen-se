Preem "En bättre resa": campaign images

Contracted by Åkestam Holst NOA to shoot the campaign images for Preem "En bättre resa" (A Better Journey), in 2023.

The images are shot at a Preem station and tell small stories from the road: a car boot packed to the roof with luggage, a family stopping to fill up on a rainy night with the pumps glowing behind them.
