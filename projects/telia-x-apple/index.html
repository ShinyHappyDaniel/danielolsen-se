Telia x Apple: product campaign images

Campaign images for Apple and Telia, shot in Stockholm in January 2026 for the agency Åkestam Holst.

The set shows the iPhone 17 Pro together with Apple Watch and AirPods, made for the Telia and Apple partnership. I worked together with the film crew that made the TV commercial for the same campaign, which is always extremely fun! But it comes with a price: an almost insanely short timeframe to do the shoot.

Clean product work with a clear commercial purpose: online and OOH.
