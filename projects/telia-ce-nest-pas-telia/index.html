Telia "Ce n’est pas Telia": still photography in Cape Town

Still photography for the Telia campaign "Ce n’est pas Telia", shot in Cape Town, South Africa.

The campaign was created by Åkestam Holst for Telia. I worked as the stills photographer alongside the film team from Giants & Toys: director Jens Jonsson, producer Calle Isberg and cinematographer Claudio Mirandas. VFX by The Chimney Pot.
