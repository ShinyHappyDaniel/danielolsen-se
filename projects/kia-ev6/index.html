KIA EV6: campaign photography with Björn Dixgård

Campaign images of the new KIA EV6 for the Swedish market, shot in Skåne in April 2025.

The campaign is a collaboration between KIA and Björn Dixgård of Mando Diao. The agency was Bizkit Havas. I was contracted to do stills and film. The location was outdoors in beautiful Skåne in southern Sweden.

The images were made for the Swedish launch. Media: outdoor, online and print.
