Marbon Collective: jewellery campaign

Campaign photos for the jewelry brand Marbon Collective.

The campaign follows two friends through a night out and the morning after. Cocktails in a dark bar, bathrobes and hair towels on a hotel bed, fries, pizza and champagne. The rings and necklaces are worn the way they would be in real life, not laid out on a table.

Hair and make-up by Nathalie Berzelius. Style and creative by Cecilie Moxheim, Filipa Eriksson and Emmelie Gustafsson. Models: Kristina Rosso and Giovanna Piccoliori.
