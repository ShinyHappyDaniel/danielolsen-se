Konstvärlden: magazine cover portraits

Contracted to shoot the cover for the Swedish art magazine Konstvärlden.

The series is a set of portraits for the magazine. Some are black and white, some in colour, and several are made together with art and objects: a print held up to the camera, a Rolleiflex, framed photographs on a gallery wall.
