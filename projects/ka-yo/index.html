Ka-Yo: outdoor fashion campaign

I am happy to be contracted by one of the most creative outdoor businesses in Sweden. Ka-Yo focuses on offering the very best equipment and clothing for active living.

We shot on location in the forest: mossy boulders, fallen logs, pine and spruce in both sun and rain. The clothes range from metallic down suits and big hooded puffer jackets to fleece sets and bucket hats, photographed the way they are meant to be used.
