Daisy Beauty Magazine: "The four seasons"

Editorials for Daisy Beauty Magazine: "The four seasons".

A beauty story in four chapters, one for each season. Close portraits with copper eyeshadow, crystal face gems, pearls and silver leaves, shot against dark, painterly backdrops.

Make-up artist and client was Mia Högfeldt. The model is Hanna Ardéhn.
