Bookbeat: European campaign images

European campaign images for Bookbeat.

The images show people listening to audiobooks in everyday moments: on the bus with rain on the window, stretched out on the sofa with headphones on, phone in hand. The set mixes these lifestyle scenes with graphic close-ups of hands and phones against a lilac background, in Bookbeat's pink and purple colours.

The campaign ran in several European markets. I was commissioned directly by Bookbeat.
