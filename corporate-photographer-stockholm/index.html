I'm Daniel Olsén, a photographer in Stockholm. I shoot corporate portraits and press photos for companies, agencies, artists and magazines, in the studio or on location at your office.

It is the same way of working as in my advertising campaigns and magazine portraits, but for your own communication.

What I shoot

  • Corporate portraits of management and staff
  • Press photos for press rooms, PR and media
  • Press photos for artists, musicians and athletes
  • Portraits for web and social media

How it works

  • We agree beforehand on how the images will be used and what they should feel like.
  • I shoot in the studio or on location at your office.
  • I do the retouch myself and deliver files that fit your channels.

Portrait work

  • Kosovare Asslani for KIA
  • Björn Dixgård of Mando Diao for the KIA EV6 campaign
  • Portrait series and cover for the art magazine Konstvärlden
  • Michael Nyqvist and Plura for Mouli

Questions and answers

Do you shoot on location at our office?

Yes, I shoot corporate portraits and press photos both in the studio and on location at the client's.

Do you do your own retouch?

Yes, I retouch my own images.

Can you shoot film at the same time?

Yes, I shoot both stills and film in the same production, and I edit and colour grade the film myself.

Do you shoot outside Stockholm?

Yes, I travel for shoots. I am based in Stockholm and work wherever the job is.
