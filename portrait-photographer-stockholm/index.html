I'm Daniel Olsén, a portrait photographer in Stockholm. I shoot portraits for companies, agencies and magazines: campaign portraits, press images and editorial portraits, in the studio or on location.

I am based in Stockholm and work wherever the job is.

What I shoot

  • Corporate portraits and press photos

Questions and answers

Do you shoot portraits for private individuals?

I mainly shoot portraits for companies, agencies and magazines. Ask anyway, I do make exceptions for private commissions.

Do you do your own retouch?

Yes, I retouch my own images. Retouch, editing and colour grading are all done in-house.

Do you travel for shoots?

Yes, I travel for shoots. I am based in Stockholm and work wherever the job is.

How do usage rights work for a portrait shoot?

Usage rights for a portrait shoot are agreed per job. Channels, period and market are written into the quote.

What is the delivery time for a portrait shoot?

The delivery time for a portrait shoot is agreed per job, so that it fits your schedule.

What does a portrait shoot cost?

The price of a portrait shoot depends on the scope of the job. You get a quote once I have seen the brief.
